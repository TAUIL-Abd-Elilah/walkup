# P1 ground truth: supporters'-club home bars (public part)

Ground truth for the P1 "Fan-Bar Test" (`docs/preregistration/p1-plan.md`): the bars that official US supporters' clubs list as the place their members watch matches. Collected once on **2026-10-13, 08:37 to 08:42 UTC** by `evals/p1/collect_bars.py`.

## What is in this folder

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `sample_20.csv` | 20 rows (7 American Outlaws, 7 Arsenal America, 6 LFC), drawn with seed 20261013, each with its source link and fetch time |
| `summary.json` | Counts per club, metro coverage, collection notes. Generated by `evals/p1/publish_sample.py` |
| `place_aliases.csv` | Hand-made city aliases used to find a Census point (neighbourhoods, regional labels, renamed places) |

The full table (426 rows) is kept out of the repository at `data/private/p1/bars_full.csv` (git-ignored). Only its SHA-256 is published, in `docs/preregistration/p1-code.sha256`.

## Coverage

- **Metro points** (Census Bureau 2026 Gazetteer, `evals/p1/centroids.py`): 418 rows matched a Census place, 7 a county subdivision (townships and boroughs such as Brick, NJ or Brooklyn, NY), 1 miss ("Manatawny", PA, an area name, not a Census place). 12 rows matched through `place_aliases.csv`, 5 through a consolidated-government name (for example Nashville-Davidson).
- **Queries**: 393 (club, metro) pairs over 260 metros: American Outlaws 227, Arsenal America 92, LFC 74.
- **Derby Test candidates**: 92 metros have bars from two or more clubs (41 with all three, 36 American Outlaws and Arsenal, 11 American Outlaws and LFC, 4 Arsenal and LFC). 37 same-metro pairs of listings look like the same bar listed by two clubs (name similarity of at least 0.85); the Derby Test excludes them.
- **Resolution to Qloo places**: run Oct 21 2026 (UTC). The coverage table in [`evals/p1/results.md`](../../evals/p1/results.md) reports it first: 343 of the 426 bars analyzed (72 did not resolve to a Qloo place, 10 were beyond 25 km, 1 had no city point). Provisional: the Google closed check did not run.

## Collection method and failures

- robots.txt was read for each host with `urllib.robotparser`; all three clubs' sites allow these pages. At most one request per second, User-Agent `HouselightsResearch/0.1 (contact via repo)` (the project's name before it became Walkup; `evals/p1/common.py`), every response cached under `data/private/p1/cache/` so re-runs do not re-fetch. 285 page requests to the three club sites and 2 Census file downloads, all HTTP 200 (plus one robots.txt read per host).
- **American Outlaws**: chapter pages are server-rendered; each location card gives the bar, its address and an "Official" mark. Only the team labels (USMNT, USWNT) are kept from the card's badges; other labels are discarded on purpose. Two chapters outside the US (London, Mexico City) are dropped. Kept as listed, with known issues: one New York City card names two bars in a single entry, and one Mississippi card's address names a different city from the chapter; both will likely fail resolution and be counted.
- **Arsenal America**: one server-rendered page. Branches with a "Main" and a "Satellite" location give two rows. The New York and Philadelphia entries point to independent organizations and list no bar.
- **LFC**: the page is rendered from embedded JSON (`__NEXT_DATA__`); the venue is written in free text. The 76 US rows were extracted by hand into a private file and the script re-checks each name against the live text on every run. Not used: 8 entries outside the US (Canada, Barbados, Bermuda), Puerto Rico, and 3 US entries that name no bar (Las Vegas, New England, New Orleans). 36 rows give a neighbourhood, landmark, town or ZIP code instead of a street address; they go through the stricter fallback resolution rule in the plan (as do 2 American Outlaws rows without a street number).

## Terms and use

These are facts (the names and listed addresses of public businesses) published by each club for the public. We store only club, chapter, city, state, bar name, address as listed, source URL, fetch time, page date when shown, and the club's own designation of the listing; nothing about any person. Each row is attributed to its source page. The full table is not redistributed: this repository publishes aggregate counts, the derived metrics and the 20-row sample above. A club that wants its rows removed from the sample can open an issue and we will remove them.
